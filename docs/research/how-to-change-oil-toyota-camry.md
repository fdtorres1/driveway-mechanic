## Scope and publication decision

Narrowed from an unsupported 2018–2023 multi-vehicle procedure to **U.S.-market 2023 Toyota Camry, non-hybrid 2.5L A25A-FKS**, specifications/preparation plus the owner's-manual oil-level check/top-up. Other years, Camry Hybrid, V6, RAV4, and Highlander are excluded. This does not validate their specifications or procedures.

The owner's manual does not establish the complete drain/filter replacement procedure or stand-support arrangement. The old under-car tutorial therefore cannot remain as verified instructions. Its drain/filter/lifting steps and torques are removed. The revised title, excerpt, metadata, opening, and body expressly describe the narrower scope. A complete oil-change tutorial remains blocked pending an applicable authoritative repair procedure, not hidden behind a disclaimer.

- Corrected primary oil from 0W-20 to 0W-16 in the narrowed 2023 scope. Removed `0w20-oil` metadata and universal 10,000-mile-only interval metadata.
- Removed broad 2018–2023 procedure, 2AR-FE claim, cross-model RAV4/Highlander fitment, “most common” assertion, and unsupported part number 90915-YZZF1/perfect-fit guarantee.
