## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Prepare the Car

1. **Run the engine for 5 minutes** to warm the oil (makes it flow better)
2. **Turn off engine and wait 10 minutes** (oil is warm but not dangerously hot)
3. **Set parking brake** and put transmission in Park
4. **Pop the hood** and locate the oil filler cap (has oil symbol)

### Step 2: Lift the Front of the Car

The **drain plug is on the passenger side** of the oil pan, accessible from the front-right wheel well.

1. **Loosen front lug nuts** (don't remove completely)
2. **Position floor jack** under the front center jack point
3. **Lift front of car** until both front wheels are off ground
4. **Place jack stands** under proper lift points (marked on frame rails)
5. **Lower car onto stands** and test stability
6. **Remove front-right wheel** for easier access

<Callout type="warning">
  **Lift points for 2018-2023 Camry:** Look for reinforced notches on the frame rails behind each wheel. Never jack on the oil pan, suspension arms, or body panels.
</Callout>

### Step 3: Locate Drain Plug and Filter

From under the front-right of the car:

- **Drain plug:** 17mm bolt on passenger side of oil pan
- **Oil filter:** Black cylindrical canister above the oil pan, toward the center

Both are accessible from this position. The drain plug may have a plastic splash shield around it that you can work through.

### Step 4: Drain the Oil

1. **Position drain pan** slightly to passenger side (oil will arc out)
2. **Remove drain plug** with 17mm socket (turn counterclockwise)
3. **Let oil drain completely** (15-20 minutes)
4. **Clean drain plug** and inspect the gasket

<Callout type="tip">
  **Pro tip:** The oil will initially shoot out, then slow to a drip. Start the drain pan to one side, then move it directly under the plug as flow slows.
</Callout>

### Step 5: Replace the Filter

While oil is draining:

1. **Position drain pan** under the filter area
2. **Remove old filter** by turning counterclockwise (may need filter wrench)
3. **Clean filter mounting surface** on engine block
4. **Apply thin coat of new oil** to new filter's rubber gasket
5. **Install new filter** hand-tight plus 3/4 turn (do NOT over-tighten)

<Callout type="warning">
  The old filter will spill oil when removed. Have your drain pan ready and expect some mess.
</Callout>

### Step 6: Reinstall Drain Plug

1. **Thread drain plug by hand** until snug (prevent cross-threading)
2. **Install new drain plug gasket** if old one is damaged
3. **Tighten with socket** to 30 ft-lbs (snug plus 1/4 turn)
4. **Don't over-tighten** — you'll strip the oil pan threads

### Step 7: Lower the Car

1. **Reinstall wheel** and hand-tighten lug nuts
2. **Raise car slightly** to remove jack stands
3. **Lower car completely**
4. **Tighten lug nuts** to spec (76 ft-lbs) in star pattern

### Step 8: Add New Oil

1. **Remove oil filler cap** on top of engine
2. **Insert funnel** into filler hole
3. **Add 4.5 quarts** of 0W-20 oil slowly
4. **Wait 5 minutes** for oil to settle
5. **Check oil level** with dipstick (should be between MIN and MAX)
6. **Add more if needed** (don't overfill)

<Callout type="info">
  **Capacity:** 2018-2023 Camry 2.5L holds 4.8 quarts total, but start with 4.5 quarts since some old oil remains in the engine.
</Callout>

### Step 9: Test and Clean Up

1. **Start engine** and let idle for 2-3 minutes
2. **Check for leaks** under the car
3. **Turn off engine** and wait 5 minutes
4. **Recheck oil level** and top off if needed
5. **Reset oil life monitor** (see owner's manual for procedure)

## Maintenance Schedule

**For 2018-2023 Camry with 0W-20 synthetic oil:**
- **Normal driving:** Every 10,000 miles
- **Severe conditions:** Every 5,000 miles

**Severe conditions include:**
- Frequent short trips (less than 5 miles)
- Stop-and-go traffic
- Extreme temperatures
- Dusty conditions
- Towing

<Callout type="tip" title="Track Your Changes">
  Keep a log of oil changes with mileage and date. This helps with warranty coverage and resale value.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-tightening drain plug** — Strips oil pan threads (expensive repair)
2. **Double-gasketing oil filter** — Causes massive leak (remove old gasket first)
3. **Overfilling with oil** — Can damage engine (check level carefully)
4. **Forgetting to reinstall drain plug** — You'll lose all new oil immediately
5. **Using wrong oil viscosity** — Stick to 0W-20 for this engine

## Troubleshooting

**Oil leak after change:**
- Check drain plug and filter tightness
- Look for double-gasketed filter
- Ensure drain plug gasket is properly seated

**Oil level too high:**
- Drain some oil through drain plug
- Never drive with overfilled oil

**Oil level too low:**
- Add small amounts and recheck
- Look for leaks if level drops quickly

## Environmental Note

**Dispose of used oil responsibly:**
- Most auto parts stores accept used oil for free
- Quick lube shops also take used oil
- Never dump oil down drains or on the ground
- Used filters should also be recycled
